# Privacy Policy – Scrobbler for radio Willy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

**What the extension reads:** the artist and title of the song playing in the Willy web player (www.willy.radio/luister), and whether the player is playing. It reads nothing on any other website.

**What it sends, and to whom:** only to Last.fm (ws.audioscrobbler.com):
- the artist, title and start time of songs you listened to for at least 30 seconds, to add them to your Last.fm profile;
- artist and title, to look up the correct spelling.

**What it stores:** on your own device only (Chrome extension storage): your Last.fm username and session key, the song currently playing, and the songs scrobbled in the last 10 minutes (to avoid duplicates). Nothing is stored on any server run by the developer; there is none.

**What it does not do:** no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data with anyone except Last.fm as described above.

**Your control:** click *Disconnect* in the extension popup or remove the extension to delete the stored session. You can also revoke access at https://www.last.fm/settings/applications. Last.fm's own handling of your data is covered by its privacy policy: https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy
